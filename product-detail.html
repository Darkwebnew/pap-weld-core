Product Overview

The PTW (Pull-Through Welding) machine is our flagship H-Beam welding platform, engineered for fabricators running continuous production of structural steel beams. Submerged arc welding gives clean, spatter-free seams with minimal post-weld cleanup.

The machine handles both vertical and horizontal beam orientations, with programmable travel speed and wire feed to match web and flange thickness on a per-job basis.

  • ✓ Continuous pull-through welding for high productivity
  • ✓ Deep penetration SAW process for structural integrity
  • ✓ Programmable settings for varying beam sizes
  • ✓ Minimal post-weld cleanup required
  • ✓ 12-month parts warranty with Chennai-based support
