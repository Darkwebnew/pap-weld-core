Our Story

From a Welding Workshop to a Robotics Manufacturer

Pap Weld Core Automation began as a specialist welding job-shop serving structural steel fabricators around Chennai. Two and a half decades of hands-on weld engineering taught us exactly where manual processes break down under tolerance and throughput pressure.

That experience became the basis for our own line of machines. Today we design, build, and commission H-Beam welding systems, robotic welding cells, and CNC fabrication equipment for structural steel, PEB, and heavy fabrication customers across India.

Every machine we ship carries the same discipline we learned on the shop floor: engineer the process first, automate it second.
