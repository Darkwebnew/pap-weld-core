Our Story

Built on the Shop Floor, Proven in the Field

Pap Weld Core Automation Private Limited was incorporated to address a clear gap in the Indian market — the absence of locally engineered, high‑performance welding automation systems that could compete with imported alternatives on both quality and cost.

Our founding team brought decades of hands‑on experience from structural steel fabrication, robotic integration, and CNC machining. We set up our manufacturing facility in Chennai — a city with deep roots in engineering and manufacturing — and built our first H‑Beam welding machine from the ground up.

Since then, we have installed systems across steel fabricators, PEB manufacturers, heavy engineering workshops, and infrastructure contractors throughout India.
