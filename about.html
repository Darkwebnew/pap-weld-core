Our Story

6+ Years of Precision Engineering

Pap Weld Core Automation Private Limited was established in Chennai with a focused mission: to deliver high-quality automation solutions for the structural steel and Pre-Engineered Building (PEB) sectors. Over the past 6+ years, we have grown into a trusted manufacturer of H-Beam welding machines, robotic welding systems, and CNC fabrication equipment.

Our hands-on engineering approach ensures every machine is built for real-world shop floor conditions — optimizing weld quality, reducing distortion, and accelerating throughput. We combine deep process knowledge with modern automation to help fabricators scale their production reliably.

Every system we deliver is engineered, built, and commissioned by our team in Chennai, with a strong emphasis on quality and post-sales support.
